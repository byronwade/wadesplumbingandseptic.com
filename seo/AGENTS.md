# SEO Evidence Instructions

- Read `../docs/seo-agent/BUILD_SPEC.md` before creating or modifying SEO records.
- Store only versionable, non-secret evidence, manifests, experiments, query ownership, link-graph data, fixtures, and evaluation artifacts here.
- Every external finding needs source URL or tool, retrieval time, scope, and a `LIVE_VERIFIED`, `MOCK_VERIFIED`, `BLOCKED_MISSING_CREDENTIALS`, or `FAILED` classification.
- Keep approved service-area facts separate from research hypotheses; hypotheses cannot authorize claims or new pages.
- Do not store analytics exports containing personal data, connector credentials, raw customer submissions, or third-party content beyond necessary short evidence excerpts.
