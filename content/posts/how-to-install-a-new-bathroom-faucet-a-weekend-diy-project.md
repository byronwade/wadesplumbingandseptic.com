A faucet replacement is manageable when the shutoffs work, the sink is accessible, and the new faucet matches the mounting pattern.

## Before buying

Confirm one-hole, centerset, or widespread mounting; measure available space; check the drain style; and inspect the shutoff valves and supply size.

## Basic sequence

1. Shut off hot and cold water and verify the faucet stops flowing.
2. Place towels and a pan below the connections.
3. Disconnect supplies and drain linkage.
4. Remove mounting hardware and clean the sink surface.
5. Install the faucet and gasket according to its instructions.
6. Connect compatible supplies without twisting or overtightening.
7. Reconnect or replace the drain assembly.
8. Remove the aerator, flush both supplies, reinstall it, and test every connection.

Stop if a shutoff does not close, a valve or pipe moves in the wall, corrosion is severe, or the sink is cracked. Those conditions can turn a cosmetic replacement into a larger leak.
