Holiday cooking and guests put more demand on drains, toilets, hot water, and septic systems. A few habits can prevent an emergency.

## Protect the kitchen drain

Cool and dispose of fats, oils, and grease in the trash. Scrape plates before rinsing. Keep bones, fibrous vegetables, coffee grounds, and expandable starches out of the disposal.

## Check toilets before guests arrive

Repair a weak flush, loose handle, running fill valve, or slow drain before use increases. Keep a flange-style plunger accessible and do not flush wipes.

## Give the water heater room to recover

Space showers and appliance use. Check for leaks, unusual noises, error codes, or insufficient hot water before the gathering.

## Protect a septic system

Spread laundry and dishwasher loads, repair leaks, and avoid a sudden day of unusually high water use. Know the last pumping and inspection dates.

If several fixtures back up, stop using water and call. Repeated flushing can make a main-line or septic backup worse.
