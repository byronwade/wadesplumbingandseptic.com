## Stop damage first

For an active leak, shut off the closest fixture valve or the main water valve. Keep electricity away from wet areas and move valuables only when it is safe.

## Find the source

Water can travel along framing and appear far from the actual failure. Meter testing, pressure isolation, moisture evidence, acoustic tools, and targeted access help narrow the source before unnecessary demolition.

The correct repair depends on pipe material, age, location, water pressure, and whether the failure is isolated. [Leak detection](/service-offerings/leak-detection) and [pipe repair](/service-offerings/pipe-repair-and-replacement) should leave you with a tested repair and a clear explanation of any larger risk.
