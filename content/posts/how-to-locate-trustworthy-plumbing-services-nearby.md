## Verify the company

Confirm the license required for the work, business identity, insurance, physical service area, and who will perform the job.

## Look for clear diagnosis

A trustworthy plumber explains the observed condition, what is known, what still needs testing, and why a specific repair is recommended.

## Review the written scope

The estimate should identify included labor, major materials, access assumptions, exclusions, testing, cleanup, permits when relevant, and payment timing.

## Treat reviews as patterns

Look for repeated comments about communication, punctuality, workmanship, problem resolution, and whether the final experience matched the estimate.

## Ask about warranties

Separate workmanship coverage from manufacturer coverage and customer-supplied equipment.

The lowest initial number is not always the lowest total cost. Compare scope, diagnosis, materials, and accountability.
