## Prepare before guests arrive

Holiday gatherings increase demand on toilets, kitchen drains, garbage disposals, and water heaters. A quick inspection several days before the event gives you time to correct small problems without an emergency call during the gathering.

## Homeowner checklist

- Run every sink and watch for slow drainage.
- Flush each toilet and confirm it stops filling.
- Check under sinks and around supply hoses for moisture.
- Test the disposal with cold water and listen for unusual sound.
- Avoid pouring grease, coffee grounds, or fibrous food into the kitchen drain.
- Space out showers, laundry, and dishwasher use if hot-water capacity is limited.

If a drain already gurgles, backs up, or repeatedly needs a plunger, schedule [drain cleaning](/service-offerings/drain-cleaning) before the extra use.
