## When repair is no longer enough

A replacement may be required when a tank is structurally unsafe, the drain field no longer treats or disperses wastewater, the property use changes, or a remodel increases design flow beyond the existing approval.

## What affects the system

Soil, slope, groundwater, setbacks, lot size, bedrooms, access, treatment requirements, power, monitoring, and local review all influence cost and schedule.
