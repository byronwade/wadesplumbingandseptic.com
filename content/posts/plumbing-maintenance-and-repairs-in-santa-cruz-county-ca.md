Routine observation helps Santa Cruz County homeowners find problems before they damage the property.

## Know the shutoffs

Locate the building main, water-heater shutoff, fixture stops, and any pump or well controls. Exercise serviceable valves periodically.

## Watch water pressure

High pressure increases wear on supplies, valves, appliances, and water heaters. Low pressure may indicate a regulator, filter, service-line, valve, or supply problem.

## Maintain the water heater

Follow the manufacturer's inspection and maintenance guidance. Watch for corrosion, relief-valve discharge, pan water, venting problems, error codes, and temperature changes.

## Pay attention to drains

Recurring slow drains deserve diagnosis. Keep grease, wipes, and damaging chemicals out of the drainage system.

## Inspect visible piping

Look for corrosion, staining, unsupported pipe, damp insulation, or mineral deposits around joints and valves.

## Keep records

Save invoices, camera videos, equipment models, permits, and prior repair locations. Good history speeds up future diagnosis.
