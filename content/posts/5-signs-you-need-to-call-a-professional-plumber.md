## 1. Several fixtures are affected

Multiple slow drains, pressure loss, or backups point to a shared part of the system.

## 2. Water is damaging the property

Active leaks, wet ceilings, soft floors, or water near electrical equipment require prompt shutoff and diagnosis.

## 3. The problem keeps returning

Repeated clogs, recurring pressure changes, or a pilot or error code that returns usually has an underlying cause.

## 4. Sewage or gas odor is present

Drain, vent, trap, sewer, septic, or fuel-gas problems can create health and safety risks. Leave unsafe areas and contact the appropriate professional.

## 5. The repair affects code or concealed systems

Water heaters, gas piping, backflow protection, underground services, septic systems, and concealed valves often require permits, testing, or specialized equipment.

Early professional diagnosis can limit damage and give you more repair options.
