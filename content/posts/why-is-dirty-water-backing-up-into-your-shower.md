## The shower may be the lowest opening

When a toilet, washing machine, or sink sends wastewater into a blocked drain, that water may appear in the lowest nearby fixture. The shower is often where the backup becomes visible even though the shower drain itself is not the cause.

## What to do

- Stop using fixtures connected to the affected drain.
- Keep people and pets away from contaminated water.
- Do not add chemical drain cleaner.
- Call for [drain cleaning](/service-offerings/drain-cleaning).

Recurring backups, root intrusion, or suspected damage may require [sewer camera inspection](/service-offerings/sewer-camera-inspection) after the line is open.
