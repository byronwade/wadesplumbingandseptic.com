## The flange is part of the drainage system

A toilet flange connects the fixture to the drain and anchors it to the floor. The correct material, elevation, solvent or mechanical connection, fasteners, and floor support all matter.

## Basic process

1. Shut off water and remove the toilet.
2. Remove the old seal and inspect the flange.
3. Confirm pipe material and damage.
4. Repair the subfloor if it cannot hold fasteners.
5. Set the replacement flange at the correct finished-floor elevation.
6. Make the approved pipe connection.
7. Secure the flange without distorting it.
8. Reset and test the toilet.

If the drain is cracked below the floor, the subfloor is damaged, or the flange connection is inaccessible, professional [toilet repair](/service-offerings/toilet-repair) can prevent a leak into the structure.
