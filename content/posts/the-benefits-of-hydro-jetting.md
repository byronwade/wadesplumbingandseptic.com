## What Is Hydro-Jetting and How Does It Work?

Hydro-jetting involves using a specialized hose that blasts water at pressures up to 4,000 PSI through your plumbing system. This high-pressure stream scours the interior of the pipes, removing grease, sludge, tree roots, mineral buildup, and other debris.

Before performing a hydro-jetting service, our licensed plumbers perform a **camera inspection** to evaluate the condition of the pipes and identify the source of the clog. This ensures targeted cleaning and prevents potential damage.

## 7 Major Benefits of Hydro-Jetting

### 1\. **Versatility**

Hydro-jetting is ideal for both **residential and commercial plumbing systems**. Whether it’s a kitchen sink, bathroom drain, or a complex main sewer line, hydro-jetting handles it all, no matter the pipe material.

### 2\. **Non-Invasive Solution**

Unlike traditional methods that may require digging up your yard or breaking through walls, hydro-jetting is **non-invasive**. This means less mess, minimal disruption to your property, and faster results.

### 3\. **Environmentally Friendly**

Hydro-jetting uses only water (no harsh chemicals or toxic cleaners), making it one of the **greenest drain cleaning methods** available. It’s safe for your pipes, your home, and the environment.

### 4\. **Extremely Effective**

Hydro-jetting removes everything from **grease and scale buildup to tree roots and heavy blockages**. It leaves your pipes practically like new, eliminating recurring clogs and restoring full flow capacity.

### 5\. **More Sanitary**

Because hydro-jetting reduces the need for manual snaking and sewer contact, it’s a **cleaner and safer solution**. The entire process minimizes exposure to hazardous waste and keeps your household protected.

### 6\. **Prevents Future Clogs**

Routine hydro-jetting acts as **preventative maintenance** , helping avoid future plumbing issues before they start. Clean pipes last longer and reduce the risk of emergency calls or pipe replacements.

### 7\. **Cost-Effective and Long-Lasting**

Compared to frequent snaking or using chemical drain cleaners, hydro-jetting is a **long-term investment** in your plumbing system. While prices vary, **hydro-jetting typically costs $350 to $600** , which is far more affordable than major pipe repairs that can run $1,200 or more.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro-Jetting

![Stacked concrete pipes for underground systems](/images/wordpress/stacked-concrete-pipes-in-an-outdoor-storage-area-surrounded-by-grass-ebc75f.webp)

**What types of systems can be hydro-jetted?**  
Hydro-jetting is safe and effective for most plumbing systems including **PVC, cast iron, and clay pipes** found in residential, commercial, and industrial settings.

**How is hydro-jetting different from traditional snaking?**
Unlike snaking, which only breaks up a portion of the blockage, hydro-jetting **completely clears the pipe walls** , preventing debris from sticking and forming new clogs.

**Is hydro-jetting safe for older pipes?**
Yes, but it must be done by experienced professionals. At Wade’s Plumbing & Septic, we inspect the pipes before jetting and adjust water pressure accordingly to **avoid damaging fragile or aging pipes**.

**How often should I schedule hydro-jetting?**
We recommend scheduling **annual or bi-annual hydro-jetting** to maintain your plumbing system, especially if you deal with recurring clogs or have older pipes.
