Water softeners and filters solve different problems. Testing the water before selecting equipment prevents buying the wrong system.

## What a softener does

Ion-exchange softeners primarily reduce calcium and magnesium hardness. They can reduce scale, spotting, soap use, and mineral buildup in water heaters and fixtures.

## What whole-house filtration does

Filter media can target sediment, chlorine, taste, odor, iron, manganese, or specific contaminants depending on the water test and equipment design.

## When a home may need both

Hard water and unwanted taste, odor, sediment, or disinfectant residual can exist together. A staged system may use sediment protection, filtration, and softening in the correct order.

## Questions to answer first

- Is the source municipal water or a private well?
- What does a current laboratory test show?
- What flow rate does the house require?
- Where will backwash and discharge water go?
- What salt, filter, and maintenance schedule is acceptable?

Choose equipment based on the water and household—not marketing claims.
