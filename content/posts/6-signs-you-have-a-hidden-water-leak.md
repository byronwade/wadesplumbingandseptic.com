Water leaks can waste thousands of gallons and damage framing, cabinets, flooring, and finishes before water becomes obvious. Watch for these six warning signs.

## 1. An unexplained water-bill increase

Compare the same season year over year. A steady increase without a change in household use can point to a toilet leak, irrigation problem, service-line leak, or concealed fixture supply.

## 2. Meter movement when everything is off

Turn off fixtures, appliances, irrigation, and ice makers. If the meter's low-flow indicator continues moving, water is still leaving the system.

## 3. Stains, soft material, or peeling paint

Yellow or brown staining, swollen trim, soft drywall, bubbling paint, or lifted flooring can indicate moisture behind the visible surface.

## 4. Musty odor or recurring mold

Moisture inside a wall or cabinet can support mold even when the leak is too small to create standing water.

## 5. Warm, damp, or unusually cool floors

Temperature differences or damp flooring can be a clue to piping under or inside the floor assembly.

## 6. Water sounds when fixtures are off

Hissing, dripping, or running-water noise in a quiet house deserves investigation.

Shut off the main water if the leak is actively causing damage. Wade's can combine pressure testing, meter isolation, acoustic equipment, thermal inspection, and targeted access to narrow down the source.
