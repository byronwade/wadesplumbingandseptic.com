Freezing water expands and can split copper, plastic, valves, filters, pumps, and fixtures.

## Before cold weather

- Disconnect hoses and protect exterior faucets.
- Insulate vulnerable piping in crawlspaces, garages, and exterior walls.
- Seal drafts near pipes without blocking required combustion air.
- Keep heat on in occupied and vacant buildings.
- Know where the main shutoff is and make sure it operates.
- Winterize irrigation, outdoor kitchens, and seasonal equipment correctly.

## During a freeze

Open cabinet doors where safe so warm air reaches piping. A small controlled drip may help in specific vulnerable systems, but it is not a substitute for insulation and heat.

## If a pipe freezes

Do not use an open flame. Shut off water if the pipe appears split, warm the area safely, and inspect as it thaws. A frozen pipe may not leak until water begins moving again.
