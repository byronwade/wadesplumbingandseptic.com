The best toilet fits the rough-in, room, users, water pressure, serviceability, and budget.

## 1. Gravity toilets

Simple, quiet, widely available, and easy to service. Bowl and trapway design matter more than appearance alone.

## 2. Pressure-assisted toilets

Compressed air strengthens the flush. These can work well in demanding or commercial settings but are louder and use specialized parts.

## 3. Dual-flush toilets

Separate flush volumes can reduce water use when the mechanism is reliable and users understand it.

## 4. Wall-hung toilets

The tank and carrier are concealed in the wall. They save floor space and simplify cleaning but require correct framing, carrier installation, and service access.

## 5. Smart or bidet-integrated toilets

Features may include washing, heated seats, drying, automatic flushing, and leak protection. Confirm electrical, water, clearances, and replacement-part support.

Measure the rough-in from the finished wall to the flange bolts, verify supply location, and compare the new base footprint with the existing floor.
