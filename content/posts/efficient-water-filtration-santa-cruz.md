## Start with the water problem you want to solve

Taste, odor, sediment, staining, hardness, and specific contaminants require different treatment. A system marketed as “whole-home filtration” may not address every condition.

## Common treatment methods

- **Sediment filtration** removes suspended particles.
- **Activated carbon** reduces many tastes, odors, and chlorine compounds.
- **Water softening** exchanges hardness minerals that create scale.
- **Reverse osmosis** provides high-quality water at selected drinking fixtures.
- **Ultraviolet treatment** can address qualifying microbiological concerns when properly designed.

Equipment also has to support the home's flow rate and water pressure. Professional [water filtration installation](/service-offerings/water-filtration-system-installation) includes correct sizing, bypass piping, drainage, flushing, and a maintenance plan.
