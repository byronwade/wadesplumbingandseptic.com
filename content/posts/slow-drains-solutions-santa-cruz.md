## One slow fixture or the whole building?

A single slow sink or tub often points to a nearby trap or branch drain. Several fixtures slowing together can indicate a larger building drain or sewer problem.

Avoid repeated chemical drain cleaners. They may not remove the actual blockage and can create a hazard for anyone opening the piping later.

Professional [drain cleaning](/service-offerings/drain-cleaning) uses access and equipment appropriate for the pipe. Recurring clogs may justify [hydro jetting](/service-offerings/hydro-jetting) or a [camera inspection](/service-offerings/sewer-camera-inspection).
