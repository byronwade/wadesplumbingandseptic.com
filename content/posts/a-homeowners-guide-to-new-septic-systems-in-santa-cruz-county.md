## Start with the property

Septic design begins with bedrooms or design flow, soil, groundwater, slope, setbacks, wells, waterways, existing improvements, and access. These conditions determine whether a conventional system is possible or advanced treatment is required.

## Typical project sequence

1. Site and records review
2. Soil evaluation and field testing
3. System design
4. Environmental-health review and permits
5. Equipment and construction planning
6. Installation and staged inspections
7. Startup, testing, and owner orientation
8. Ongoing maintenance and monitoring

An [alternative septic system](/service-offerings/alternative-septic-system-installation) can make difficult properties usable, but the equipment and dispersal method must match the approved design.
