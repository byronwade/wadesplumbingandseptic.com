Start by identifying whether one fixture or several fixtures are affected. Multiple slow drains, gurgling toilets, or backup at the lowest fixture can indicate a main-line or septic problem.

## For a single fixture

1. Remove visible hair or debris from the stopper.
2. Use the correct cup or flange plunger and block nearby overflow openings.
3. Clean an accessible sink trap after placing a pan below it.
4. Stop if the piping is damaged, glued incorrectly, or difficult to reassemble.

Avoid mixing chemical cleaners. They can damage piping, create hazardous reactions, and expose the technician who later opens the drain.

## Call a professional when

- Several fixtures are affected
- The clog returns repeatedly
- Sewage backs up
- Roots or damaged pipe are suspected
- A cable cannot pass
- The property uses a septic system and symptoms extend beyond one fixture

Professional cable equipment, cameras, and hydro jetting each solve different conditions. Repeated clearing without diagnosis may only create a temporary opening.
