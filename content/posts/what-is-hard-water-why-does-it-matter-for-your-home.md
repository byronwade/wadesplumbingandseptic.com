## Hard water contains dissolved minerals

Calcium and magnesium are the primary hardness minerals. They are not usually a health emergency, but they can leave spots, reduce soap performance, create scale, and shorten the efficient life of water-heating and plumbing equipment.

## Signs of hard water

- White scale on faucets and showerheads
- Spots on glass and dishes
- Soap that does not lather or rinse easily
- Dry-feeling skin or laundry
- Scale inside water heaters and appliances

Water testing helps determine hardness and whether a softener or another [water treatment system](/service-offerings/water-filtration-system-installation) is appropriate.
