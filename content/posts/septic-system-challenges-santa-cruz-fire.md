## Post-fire systems need a careful inspection

Heat and direct fire can damage plastic components, controls, wiring, lids, risers, and shallow piping. Firefighting water, erosion, debris, and heavy equipment can also disturb the tank or drain field.

## Before rebuilding or increasing use

- Keep vehicles and equipment off the tank and drain field.
- Identify damaged lids, exposed piping, alarms, and control panels.
- Watch for settlement, sewage odor, surfacing liquid, or unusual vegetation.
- Confirm permits and approved design before altering the system.

A professional [septic inspection](/service-offerings/septic-tank-inspection-and-assessment) can document current conditions and determine whether repair, pumping, electrical work, or a redesigned system is needed.
