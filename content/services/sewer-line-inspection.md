## Understand the condition of the building sewer

An inspection can combine fixture testing, cleanout review, camera work, locating, and evaluation of visible piping or site symptoms.

We document important findings and distinguish routine maintenance concerns from defects that may require cleaning, repair, replacement, or additional access.
