## Restore even distribution

The distribution box divides effluent among drain-field lines. Settlement, root intrusion, damaged outlets, or a broken box can overload part of the field while leaving other sections underused.

We expose and evaluate the box, verify elevations, repair or replace damaged components, and balance flow where the system design allows.
