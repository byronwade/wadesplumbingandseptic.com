## Prepare drainage before heavy rain

Restricted storm drainage can flood landscaping, foundations, garages, parking areas, and low points around a building.

We locate available access, remove the obstruction with appropriate equipment, verify discharge where visible, and identify damaged or poorly graded sections that cleaning alone cannot correct.
