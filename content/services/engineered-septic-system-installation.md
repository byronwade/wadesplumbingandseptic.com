## What is an engineered septic system?

An engineered (or alternative) septic system uses advanced treatment and dispersal when a standard gravity system cannot meet county standards. Common options include:

- **Aerobic Treatment Units (ATUs):** Compact treatment that produces cleaner effluent for difficult sites
- **Mound systems:** Built-up sand beds where native soil is too shallow
- **Pressure distribution and drip systems:** Even dosing across limited or irregular drainfields
- **Recirculating filters and media filters:** Extra treatment before dispersal
- **Hybrid designs:** Combinations tailored to the parcel, setbacks, and soil report

County Environmental Health requirements and a licensed designer or engineer drive the final design. We work with that process every week.

## Cost and expectations

Engineered systems cost more than a simple tank-and-field replacement because of treatment equipment, design, and site work. Ranges vary widely by system type, access, and soil conditions. We provide transparent estimates after we understand the site, and we separate what is known from what depends on engineering or county review.

Ongoing maintenance matters: filters, pumps, blowers, and service contracts keep advanced systems healthy. We set that expectation up front.
