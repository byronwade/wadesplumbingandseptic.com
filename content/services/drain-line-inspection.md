## Find where the drainage problem begins

Interior drain issues may involve a fixture branch, vent, trap, fitting, main line, or damaged concealed pipe.

We review which fixtures are affected, inspect available access, test flow, and use camera or other diagnostic methods where they can provide useful evidence.
