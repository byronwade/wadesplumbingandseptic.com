## Finish the installation cleanly and correctly

Fixture work requires more than connecting a few hoses. Mounting, sealing, water pressure, drainage alignment, stops, traps, trim, and manufacturer requirements all affect the finished result.

We inspect the fixture and existing rough-in, identify compatibility issues, complete the authorized installation, and test operation and visible connections.
