## Understand the condition of the system

A septic inspection evaluates accessible tanks, lids, baffles, filters, visible piping, alarms, pumps, controls, and the drain-field area as applicable to the system type and inspection scope.

## Clear findings, not vague guesses

We document observed conditions, explain maintenance needs, and identify items that may require repair, pumping, further testing, or review by the local authority.
