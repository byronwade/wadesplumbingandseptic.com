## Make a durable repair

We locate the affected section, consider pipe material and surrounding conditions, isolate the system, remove failed material, and install an appropriate repair.

After restoring service, we test the work, inspect accessible connections, and explain whether the failure appears isolated or part of a larger piping condition.
