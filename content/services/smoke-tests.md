## Find odor paths and concealed defects

Smoke testing can reveal open fittings, cracked piping, failed seals, abandoned connections, and vent defects that are difficult to locate visually.

We isolate the test area, introduce smoke under controlled pressure, inspect likely leak paths, and document locations that require access or repair.
