## Documentation backed by a real inspection

Certification requirements vary by jurisdiction and transaction. We confirm the requested scope, inspect applicable system components, and provide clear findings for the owner, buyer, agent, lender, or local authority.

If repairs or additional testing are needed, those items are explained before certification is completed.
