## Restore a safe, watertight tank

Cracked tanks, failed baffles, deteriorated lids, leaking penetrations, and structural damage can affect treatment and create safety hazards.

We inspect the accessible condition, determine whether a durable repair is allowed, and provide a replacement plan when the tank can no longer be safely restored.
