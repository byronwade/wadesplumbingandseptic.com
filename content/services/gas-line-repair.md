## Gas leaks require immediate care

If you smell gas, leave the area, avoid switches and ignition sources, and contact the gas provider or emergency services from a safe location.

For authorized repair work, we isolate the affected piping, remove failed material, install listed components, and complete the required pressure or leak testing before returning the system to service.
