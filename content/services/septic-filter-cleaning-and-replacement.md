## Protect the most expensive part of the system

The effluent filter helps keep suspended solids from reaching the drain field. When it becomes restricted, fixtures may drain slowly or the tank level may rise.

We safely access and clean the filter, inspect it for damage, reinstall it correctly, and explain an appropriate maintenance interval.
