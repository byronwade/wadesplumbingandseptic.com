## Keep treatment equipment operating efficiently

Salt bridging, resin fouling, restricted injectors, incorrect settings, worn seals, and drainage problems can reduce softener performance.

We review symptoms and water condition, inspect accessible components, perform the agreed maintenance, and explain settings, salt use, and future service needs.
