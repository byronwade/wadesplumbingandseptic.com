## Restore dependable operation

Toilet problems can come from fill and flush components, water pressure, a partial blockage, a damaged flange, a failed seal, or the fixture itself.

We identify the actual cause, explain repair and replacement options, complete the authorized work, and test fill, shutoff, flush, drainage, and visible connections.
