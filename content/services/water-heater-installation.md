## Signs You Need a New Water Heater

Recognizing the signs that your water heater may need replacement can save you from unexpected failures and costly repairs. Common indicators include inconsistent water temperatures, unusual noises, leaks, and increased energy bills. If you're experiencing any of these issues, it might be time to consider a new water heater installation.

## Preventing Future Water Heater Issues

Regular maintenance and timely repairs can extend the lifespan of your water heater. Consider investing in water softeners to combat hard water issues, which can lead to sediment buildup. Additionally, routine inspections can help identify potential problems before they become major issues.
