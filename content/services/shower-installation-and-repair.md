## Restore temperature, flow, and leak-free operation

Shower symptoms can come from cartridges, pressure balance components, stops, valve depth, piping, seals, drains, or waterproofing outside the plumbing scope.

We identify the plumbing condition, explain access and finish limitations, complete the agreed repair or installation, and test hot, cold, drainage, and visible connections.
