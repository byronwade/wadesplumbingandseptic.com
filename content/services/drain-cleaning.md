## Restore flow without guessing

Hair, soap, grease, paper, roots, scale, damaged pipe, and improper drainage can create similar symptoms. We identify the affected fixtures and choose the cleaning method that fits the access and piping.

If the blockage returns or the equipment reveals damage, we explain the next inspection or repair option instead of treating repeated cleaning as a permanent fix.
