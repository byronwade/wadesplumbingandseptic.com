## Remove accumulated grease and solids

Routine cleaning protects downstream piping and helps a kitchen avoid backups, odor, and enforcement issues.

We access the interceptor, remove accumulated material through the appropriate service process, inspect accessible baffles and connections, and report visible damage or maintenance concerns.
