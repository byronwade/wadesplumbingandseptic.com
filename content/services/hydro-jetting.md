## Clean the full inside diameter of the pipe

Hydro jetting can remove buildup that a cable opens through without fully cleaning. It is especially useful for recurring grease, sludge, and root intrusion.

Pipe condition and access matter. We evaluate whether jetting is safe, select the correct nozzle and pressure, control downstream debris, and recommend camera verification when needed.
