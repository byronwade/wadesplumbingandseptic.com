## Signs You Need Water Filtration System Installation in Santa Cruz County

Identifying the need for a water filtration system can be straightforward if you know what to look for. Here are some common signs:

- **Unpleasant Taste or Odor:** If your water has a metallic taste or sulfur smell, it may indicate high mineral content or contamination.
- **Stains and Deposits:** Hard water can leave mineral deposits on fixtures and stains on clothing.
- **Health Concerns:** Skin irritation or stomach issues can arise from certain water contaminants.
