## Clean the pipe with controlled water pressure

High-pressure jetting can remove grease, sludge, roots, and accumulated material that cable equipment may leave behind.

We first consider pipe condition, access, downstream components, and the location of the restriction so cleaning does not move a problem into a more sensitive part of the system.
