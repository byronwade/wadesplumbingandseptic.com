## Understanding the Process and Timeline

### The Trenchless Replacement Process

Our trenchless sewer line replacement process involves two main methods: pipe bursting and pipe lining. In pipe bursting, a new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking the old pipe apart. In pipe lining, a resin-coated liner is inserted into the existing pipe, creating a new pipe within the old one. Both methods are efficient and effective, ensuring minimal disruption.

The timeline for a trenchless sewer line replacement varies based on the complexity of the project. Typically, it can be completed in just a few days, much quicker than traditional methods.
