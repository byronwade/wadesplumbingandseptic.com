## Repair the sewer with less property disruption

Trenchless methods can avoid a full-length open trench through landscaping, driveways, or hardscape. A camera inspection and site evaluation determine whether the existing alignment and pipe condition are suitable.

The right method depends on access, collapse, offsets, diameter, depth, connections, soil, and local requirements. We explain when trenchless work is practical and when excavation is the safer long-term solution.
