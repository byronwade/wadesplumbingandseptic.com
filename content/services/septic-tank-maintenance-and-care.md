## Understanding Septic Tank Maintenance: What It Involves

Septic tank maintenance involves a series of inspections, cleanings, and adjustments that keep your system running smoothly. Our maintenance program includes:

- Thorough tank inspection
- Effluent filter cleaning and replacement
- Riser and lid checks
- Pump and alarm testing for advanced systems
- Drainfield evaluation

Regular maintenance extends the life of your septic system, prevents blockages, and ensures compliance with local regulations. For more detailed guidance, visit our [septic tank care and maintenance guide](/proper-care-and-maintenance-of-your-septic-tank-a-comprehensive-guide).

## Signs You Need Septic Tank Maintenance

Recognizing the signs of a failing septic system can save you from costly repairs. Look for:

- Slow draining sinks and toilets
- Unpleasant odors around the septic tank or drainfield
- Standing water in the yard
- Gurgling sounds in the plumbing system

If you notice any of these signs, it's crucial to schedule a septic tank inspection promptly. Our [septic tank inspection and assessment](/service-offerings/septic-tank-inspection-and-assessment) services can diagnose and address issues before they escalate.

### Causes of Septic System Failures

Several factors can lead to septic system failures, including improper maintenance, excessive water usage, and flushing non-biodegradable items. Understanding these causes helps in preventing future issues and maintaining a healthy septic system.
