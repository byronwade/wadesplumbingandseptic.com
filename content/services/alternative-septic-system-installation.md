## Engineered solutions for difficult properties

Alternative systems can combine aerobic treatment units, media filters, dosing pumps, pressure distribution, drip dispersal, disinfection, controls, and remote monitoring.

## A complete installation process

We coordinate the approved design, permits, equipment, excavation, tanks, piping, electrical and control requirements, testing, inspections, startup, and owner education. The exact system is selected for the site and regulatory requirements—not from a one-size-fits-all package.

## When advanced treatment makes sense

- Steep hillside terrain
- Poor or shallow soil
- High groundwater
- Limited lot area or difficult setbacks
- Properties near environmentally sensitive areas
- Sites where a conventional system has been denied
