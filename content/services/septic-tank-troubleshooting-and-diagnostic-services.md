## Find the failure before replacing parts

Septic symptoms can come from the building drain, tank, filter, pump, float, control panel, distribution system, or soil absorption area.

We follow the system from the building toward the field, test accessible components, and explain which condition is confirmed, which is suspected, and what the next repair step should be.
