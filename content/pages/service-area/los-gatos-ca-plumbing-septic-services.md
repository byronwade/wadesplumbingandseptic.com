## Septic services near Los Gatos

Los Gatos is predominantly sewer-served through municipal and district systems, especially in flatter neighborhoods closer to town. However, hillside and mountain-adjacent parcels near the Santa Cruz County border may still operate on **private septic** - particularly on larger lots, legacy properties, and addresses that were rural before suburban growth reached them.

For properties we confirm are in our service area, septic support may include:

- Tank pumping and routine maintenance
- Inspections for real estate transactions
- Effluent filter service, risers, and alarm troubleshooting
- Leach field evaluation on sloped, wooded parcels
- Repair and replacement planning under applicable county permit rules

Santa Clara County permitting applies to septic work on the Santa Clara side of the line. We will clarify jurisdiction and requirements when we confirm your address.

If your property is on sewer, septic services do not apply - but lateral line inspection and drain cleaning for backups still may. We verify your system type on site.

## Local conditions near the county line

The Los Gatos foothills transition quickly from suburban streets to oak woodland and steep terrain. Soil conditions, access roads, and infrastructure vary block by block. A neighbor two streets away may be on a completely different water and waste system than your property.

Tree root intrusion, ground movement on slopes, and aging cast-iron or clay sewer laterals are common issues in older hillside homes. Winter rain saturates soils and can expose marginal septic drainfields or worsen sewer lateral problems that stay hidden in dry months.

Properties near the border often feel more like Santa Cruz Mountain communities than Silicon Valley suburbs. That is the geography Wade's is built for - and why our Los Gatos coverage is intentional and limited rather than county-wide.
