## When the house won't drain, and it's not just a clog

Ben Lomond homeowners often describe the same frustration: every drain in the house gurgles, but the problem isn't always inside the walls. On septic, a saturated drainfield on a shaded slope looks a lot like a main-line blockage until you inspect both.

Our plumbing team handles:

- **Sewer and drain repairs** on older ABS, cast iron, and Orangeburg pipe common in 1970s to 80s builds
- **Camera inspections** to separate root breaks from collapsed lines before excavation
- **Water pressure problems** on long well-fed runs, especially homes at the end of a shared drive
- **Winterization and freeze damage** in unheated guest units and garage conversions
- **Fixture and water heater work** matched to your actual flow rate, not a catalog spec

Redwood roots don't respect property lines. In Ben Lomond, they're often the reason a "small leak" becomes a half-day dig. We locate the break, repair it, and talk honestly about whether root barriers or line reroutes make sense for your lot.

## Septic systems built into the hillside

Nearly every Ben Lomond parcel relies on onsite wastewater treatment. Percolation rates change with elevation, even neighboring lots can behave differently. Systems installed decades ago may be undersized for today's household or an added ADU.

Wade's septic services include:

- **Tank pumping and cleaning** with clear notes on what we found (sludge depth, baffles, inlet/outlet condition)
- **County inspections and certifications** when you're buying, selling, or refinancing
- **Filter service, risers, and alarm checks**, small upgrades that prevent expensive emergencies
- **Leach field evaluation** when wet spots appear downhill from the tank or toilets back up during laundry
- **System repair and replacement**, including [engineered septic designs](/engineered-septic-systems-santa-cruz-county) when Ben Lomond's slopes won't support a standard gravity layout

If your property was affected by wildfire rebuild requirements, septic setbacks and water supply upgrades often go hand in hand. We coordinate both sides so you're not solving plumbing in a vacuum.

## The Ben Lomond details that matter

**Access.** Fire roads, single-lane bridges, and tight turnarounds affect whether a pump truck can reach your tank on the first visit. We plan for that.

**Wells.** Many homes draw from private wells with iron, manganese, or fine sediment that clogs aerators and showerheads. Plumbing symptoms sometimes start at the source, not the fixture.

**Occupancy.** Ben Lomond has a strong community of long-term residents mixed with weekend retreats. Septic loading and freeze risk differ between the two; we adjust maintenance advice accordingly.
