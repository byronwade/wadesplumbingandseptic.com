Felton sits where Highway 9 bends into the redwoods, close enough to Scotts Valley for groceries, far enough up the San Lorenzo Valley that most properties still run on septic, wells, and long supply lines buried under roots. Wade's Plumbing & Septic is a family-owned Santa Cruz County shop that knows this corridor: the mix of full-time homes near downtown, older cabins off Graham Hill Road, and hillside lots where a "simple" leak turns into a trench through clay and redwood feeder roots.

## Plumbing on Felton's mix of old and new

Felton plumbing is rarely one-size-fits-all. Victorian-era cottages near the village may still have galvanized supply lines corroding from the inside. Newer construction off East Zayante or toward Mount Hermon often shares a well system with sediment that clogs fixtures and wears out tankless heaters faster than coastal homes.

We handle the work Felton homeowners actually call about:

- **Well supply issues**, low pressure at the far end of the house, air in lines, sediment at taps
- **Freeze and burst risk**, canyon shade keeps pipes cold; hose bibs and crawlspaces need attention before winter storms
- **Root intrusion**, redwoods and Douglas fir find sewer laterals; we camera, locate, and repair without guessing
- **Water heaters**, tank and tankless installs sized for well flow rates, not city pressure
- **Drain cleaning and hydro-jetting**, kitchen and main lines on properties that have seen decades of use

If water is backing up, we diagnose whether the problem is plumbing or septic before recommending a fix. That distinction matters on every Felton call.

## Septic is the default here, not the exception

Most Felton parcels are on individual septic systems. Tank size, soil percolation, and setback distances vary block by block. Some systems date to the 1960s; others were replaced after the CZU Lightning Complex fires reshaped rebuild plans uphill.

Wade's septic work in Felton includes:

- Routine **pumping and cleaning** with honest intervals based on household size, not a calendar sales pitch
- **Inspections and certifications** for real estate, refinancing, or county requirements
- **Riser installs, alarms, and effluent filters** so you can maintain the system without digging up the yard each time
- **Drainfield troubleshooting**, surfacing effluent, slow drains throughout the house, or odors near the leach area
- **Repairs and replacements**, including coordination with [engineered septic systems](/service-offerings/engineered-septic-system-installation) when Felton's slopes and setbacks demand an alternative design

Steep lots near the river corridor can limit conventional leach field placement. We explain what the county will approve and what your property can support, before you spend money on a plan that won't pass.

## What makes Felton different from "just up the hill"

Proximity to Roaring Camp and Henry Cowell brings seasonal traffic and part-time occupancy, which changes how septic systems load. A cabin used only on weekends behaves differently than a family of five year-round. We factor that into maintenance schedules and system evaluations.

Felton also sits at the junction of several micro-climates: fog lingers in the lower river flats while ridges dry out early. That affects where soils perk and where roots stay aggressive year-round. Local experience beats a generic checklist.
