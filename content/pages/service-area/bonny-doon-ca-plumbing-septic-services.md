## Plumbing at the end of the road

Bonny Doo calls look different from Felton or Santa Cruz city jobs:

**Access and logistics.** Narrow roads, limited turnaround, and gates mean we confirm truck and equipment needs before dispatch. A water heater swap isn't hard; getting the old tank down the hill can be.

**Wells and storage.** Many Bonny Doon homes depend on wells with storage tanks, booster pumps, and filtration for iron or sediment. Low pressure at the master bath might be a failing pump three hundred feet uphill.

**Fire rebuild upgrades.** CZU losses brought new construction with modern plumbing codes, often tied to older site utilities. We reconcile what's new inside the house with what's still in the ground from the previous structure.

**Root intrusion and long laterals.** Forested lots mean sewer runs through root territory. Camera inspection is standard, not optional.

**Seasonal dryness.** Hard soil shifts. Pipe joints stress. Leaks appear after the first real rain.

We handle leak detection, repipes, drain cleaning, sewer repair, water heaters, and well supply troubleshooting sized for Bonny Doon reality.

## Septic on slopes that reject easy answers

Flat leach fields are rare on Bonny Doon ridges. Percolation varies within a single parcel. Vineyards and orchard land add setback questions. Many systems are decades old; some were never permitted to today's standards.

Wade's septic services:

- **Pumping and maintenance** on schedules that match occupancy, full-time homes vs. weekend land
- **Locating and documenting** tanks when ownership changed and records didn't
- **Inspections and certifications** for a buyer pool that knows Bonny Doon complexity
- **Risers, filters, alarms** to make future service possible without landscape archaeology
- **Drainfield failure assessment**, surfacing, odors, persistent backups
- **Repair and replacement**, heavily weighted toward [engineered septic systems in Santa Cruz County](/service-offerings/engineered-septic-system-installation) when gravity layouts won't work

An undersized or failed system isn't a footnote on a rural property. It's a gate on resale and rebuilding. We tell you what the county will require before you hire a designer.
