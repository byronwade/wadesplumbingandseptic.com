## Plumbing where the valley gets steeper

Boulder Creek plumbing calls tend to cluster around a few real problems:

### Wells and long supply runs

Homes set back from the road often have hundreds of feet of buried supply line. A leak underground can run for weeks before the bill—or the mud—gives it away. We pressure-test, locate, and repair without tearing up more yard than necessary.

Sediment from wells clogs valves, ruins washers, and shortens water heater life. We install filtration where it helps and fix the symptoms when the source is manageable.

### Roots, age, and fire-era infrastructure

Pre-1980s pipe, improvised repairs from prior owners, and root masses in sewer laterals are common. Redwood and fir roots follow moisture—sewer lines are a map.

After fire rebuilds, many Boulder Creek homes have new interior plumbing tied to older exterior laterals or shared driveways. We trace the full path before quoting work.

### Cold-weather reality

Upstream canyons freeze harder than Felton. Hose bibs, crawlspace pipes, and uninsulated guest units need attention. We repair burst lines and help homeowners prevent the next freeze.

## Septic: the backbone of Boulder Creek living

If you're on city sewer in Boulder Creek, you're the exception. Most parcels depend on a tank-and-leach-field system sized for soils that can change within a single acre.

**Pumping and maintenance.** Tanks buried on slopes still need regular pumping. We document levels and condition so you know whether you're on a three-year cycle or need to watch it closer.

**Failed or failing drainfields.** Boulder Creek's combination of clay, rock, and slope pushes effluent where it shouldn't go. Surfacing in the yard, odors after laundry, or backups when the dishwasher runs are warning signs—not quirks to live with.

**Permits and engineered systems.** When a standard leach field won't fit or won't perk, Santa Cruz County often requires an engineered alternative. We work with [engineered septic system](/engineered-septic-systems-santa-cruz-county) designers and installers who understand mountain lots—not flat farmland specs copied onto a hillside.

**Real estate and compliance.** Buying or selling? We provide inspections and certifications that reflect what inspectors actually look for in the SLV.

## Boulder Creek isn't Ben Lomond with a different zip code

Farther from the bay means cooler nights, wetter winters in the canyons, and longer response distances for every trade.
