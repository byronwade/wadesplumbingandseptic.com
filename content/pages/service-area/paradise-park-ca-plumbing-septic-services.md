Paradise Park follows the San Lorenzo River, a narrow ribbon of homes and cabins between Felton and the forest, where the road shares space with redwoods and the sound of water is never far away. Some places are year-round residences; many are weekend retreats that wake up in summer and go quiet in winter. That pattern shapes every plumbing and septic decision Wade's Plumbing & Septic makes here.

We're a family-owned Santa Cruz County company. Paradise Park isn't a volume market for us. It's a community where one backed-up toilet can ruin a holiday, and a septic tank that wasn't pumped before the rainy season can turn a river-adjacent lot into a problem nobody wants.

## River-corridor plumbing

Paradise Park homes skew older and smaller. Plumbing reflects decades of mountain use:

- **Galvanized supply pipe** still hiding in walls, restricting flow and failing at threads
- **Small water heaters** undersized after a kitchen remodel or an added shower
- **Drain lines** clogged with decades of use, or roots from riparian trees that love sewer moisture
- **Hose bibs and exterior fixtures** exposed to humidity and occasional hard freezes in the canyon
- **Power outages** during winter storms, well pumps stop if you rely on a private supply upslope

We repair leaks, clear drains, replace heaters, and camera sewer lines before recommending excavation. In a tight community where driveways are short and yards are precious, that matters.

## Septic beside the San Lorenzo

Paradise Park is entirely on **onsite septic**. Proximity to the river corridor means setbacks, soil conditions, and seasonal groundwater deserve respect, not alarm, but honest assessment.

**Seasonal occupancy.** A cabin used eight weekends a year still needs periodic pumping; biological activity doesn't pause because you're in San Jose. Conversely, over-pumping a lightly used tank wastes money. We recommend intervals based on how the home is actually used.

**Aging infrastructure.** Original tanks and leach fields from the 1950s to 70s are still in the ground on some lots. Baffles corrode. Concrete spalls. Risers were never installed. We document condition so owners know whether they're maintaining or budgeting for replacement.

**Wet winters.** Saturated soils reduce drainfield capacity. Slow drains in February aren't always a clog. They can be a full leach line. We distinguish septic failure from plumbing blockage.

**Permits and upgrades.** When replacement is unavoidable, Santa Cruz County reviews designs carefully near sensitive drainage. We work with [engineered septic system](/service-offerings/engineered-septic-system-installation) options when standard layouts won't clear.

Services include pumping, cleaning, inspections, certifications, filter service, riser installs, drainfield evaluation, and system repair or replacement.
