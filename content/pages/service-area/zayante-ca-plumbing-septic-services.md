Zayante doesn't show up on every map the same way twice. The name covers a scatter of homes off East Zayante Road, Old Felton Road, and the side lanes that branch into forest - places where GPS confidence drops and the plumber you hire needs to know how to find a tank lid under duff and fir needles.

Wade's Plumbing & Septic is a family-owned Santa Cruz County operation that treats Zayante as what it is: rural San Lorenzo Valley fringe, not a suburb with septic as an afterthought. Wells, long driveways, and systems installed when the county's rules looked different are the norm.

## Out here, plumbing starts at the well

Zayante service calls often begin with "we have no pressure" or "the water looks rusty again." City-water assumptions don't apply.

- **Well pumps and pressure tanks** - short cycling, failure after power outages, tanks waterlogged from a torn bladder
- **Sediment and mineral loading** - iron staining, clogged aerators, premature water heater failure
- **Long buried supply lines** - leaks that show up as a green patch in summer or a sinkhole near the oak tree
- **Sewer laterals through root zones** - camera inspection before anyone brings in a backhoe
- **Freeze damage** in canyon-bottom homes where cold air pools overnight

Many Zayante properties include detached workshops, guest cabins, or rental units added over time. Each addition stresses a system that wasn't designed for it. We map what you have before suggesting upgrades.

## Septic where "out of sight" becomes expensive

Zayante parcels are heavily wooded. Tanks get buried on slopes with limited leach field options. Maintenance deferred because the lid is hard to find becomes a full drainfield replacement later.

Wade's septic services:

- **Locating and pumping** tanks that haven't been serviced in years - we expose lids, document depth, and establish a baseline
- **Real estate inspections** when buyers want clarity on a system with no paper trail
- **Filter, baffle, and riser work** so the next pump doesn't start with a shovel
- **Leach field evaluation** for surfacing effluent, persistent odors, or backups that don't respond to drain cleaning
- **Repairs and replacements**, including [engineered septic systems](/engineered-septic-systems-santa-cruz-county) when Zayante's slopes and tree preservation limits rule out a standard layout

Forest fire history in the wider SLV changed how some owners think about access roads and water storage. Septic and well work often intersect with rebuild planning - we coordinate both.
