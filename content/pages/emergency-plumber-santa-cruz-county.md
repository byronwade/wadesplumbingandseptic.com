## Call first for the fastest response

Burst pipes, active leaks, sewage backups, failed pumps, and overflowing fixtures can quickly damage a property. Call 831.225.4344 so the team can confirm the service city, understand the immediate risk, and recommend safe shutoff steps while scheduling the fastest available response.

### Urgent problems we help with

- Burst or leaking water lines
- Main sewer and drain backups
- Failed water heaters and active tank leaks
- Septic alarms, backups, and surfacing wastewater
- Failed shutoff valves, pumps, and pressure equipment

If water is near electrical equipment, keep people away and contact the appropriate emergency authority or utility.
