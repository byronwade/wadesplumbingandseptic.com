## Plumbing questions

### How can I prevent frozen pipes?

Insulate exposed pipes, keep cabinet doors open during cold snaps, let faucets drip when temperatures drop, and keep indoor heat on. See [expert tips](/expert-tips) for a longer winter checklist.

### What should I do if I have a pipe leak?

Shut off water at the fixture or the main valve. Contain the water if it is safe. Then call [831.225.4344](tel:+18312254344) during business hours. Do not do electrical work near standing water.

## Septic questions

### Do you pump septic tanks?

No. Wade's Plumbing & Septic does not pump septic tanks. We install and repair septic systems and work with your engineer on [engineered septic systems](/service-offerings/engineered-septic-system-installation). Hire a pumping company for routine pumping.

### How often should I pump my septic tank?

Most homes pump every 3 to 5 years. Household size, water use, and system design change that interval. Wade's does not pump tanks. We can inspect the system and tell you what we see.

### What are signs of septic system problems?

Slow drains, gurgling fixtures, sewage odors, soggy spots over the drainfield, a patch of unusually green grass, and backups. Call for an inspection if you see those signs.
