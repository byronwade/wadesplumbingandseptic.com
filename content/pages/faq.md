## Plumbing Questions

### How can I prevent frozen pipes?

Insulate exposed pipes, keep cabinet doors open during cold snaps, let faucets drip when temperatures drop, and keep indoor heat on. For a deeper checklist, see our [expert tips](/expert-tips) on winter plumbing prep.

### What should I do if I have a pipe leak?

Shut off water to the fixture or at the main valve, contain the water if it is safe to do so, then call us at [831.225.4344](tel:+18312254344) during business hours. Avoid DIY electrical work near standing water.

## Septic Questions

### Do you pump septic tanks?

No. Wade's Plumbing & Septic installs septic systems, working alongside engineers on [engineered septic systems](/service-offerings/engineered-septic-system-installation), and does not offer septic tank pumping.

### How often should I pump my septic tank?

Most residential tanks need pumping every 3 to 5 years, depending on household size, water use, and system design. We can inspect your tank and recommend a schedule that fits your property.

### What are signs of septic system problems?

Slow drains, gurgling fixtures, sewage odors, soggy spots over the drainfield, unusually green grass in one area, and backups are common warnings. Contact us for an inspection before a small issue becomes a full system failure.
