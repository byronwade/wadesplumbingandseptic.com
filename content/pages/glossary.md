## Plumbing terms

### Backflow preventer

A device that keeps contaminated water from reversing direction and entering the potable water supply. Common on irrigation, commercial kitchens, and some water heaters. See [backflow prevention testing](/service-offerings/backflow-prevention-testing).

### Cleanout

A capped access point that allows a drain or sewer line to be inspected and cleaned. If yours is buried under landscaping, we can locate and raise access during service.

### DWV

Drain, waste, and vent piping that moves wastewater away while balancing air pressure in the drainage system. Vent issues often show up as gurgling or slow fixtures even when the drain is mostly clear.

### Hydro jetting

High-pressure water cleaning that scours grease, scale, and soft roots from a pipe wall. Different from snaking, which mainly punches a hole through a clog. See [hydro jetting](/service-offerings/hydro-jetting).

### PRV

A pressure-reducing valve that lowers incoming water pressure to a safe operating range. High street pressure shortens fixture and heater life on some Santa Cruz County services.

### T&P relief valve

The temperature-and-pressure safety valve installed on a tank-style water heater. Discharge piping must terminate safely; never cap this valve.

### Trenchless sewer repair

Methods that rehabilitate or replace a buried pipe with limited excavation, such as pipe lining or pipe bursting. Useful when driveways and mature trees make open-cut expensive. See [trenchless sewer line replacement](/service-offerings/trenchless-sewer-line-replacement).

## Septic terms

### ATU

An aerobic treatment unit that uses oxygen and biological treatment to produce cleaner effluent than a basic septic tank. Often part of engineered solutions on difficult lots. See [engineered septic systems](/service-offerings/engineered-septic-system-installation).

### Distribution box

A component that divides septic effluent among multiple drain-field lines. A settled or damaged box can overload one line and leave others dry.

### Drain field

The soil absorption area where treated septic effluent is dispersed. Also called a leach field. Surfacing effluent or unusually green strips are warning signs.

### Effluent filter

A removable filter at the tank outlet that reduces solids entering the drain field. Cleaning it during pump-outs protects the field. See [septic filter cleaning](/service-offerings/septic-filter-cleaning-and-replacement).

### Perc test

A percolation test that measures how quickly soil accepts water. Failed or marginal perc is a common reason mountain and ridge parcels need engineered designs.

### Riser

A watertight extension that brings a septic-tank access opening closer to the ground surface so pumping and inspections do not require digging every visit. See [septic tank riser installation](/service-offerings/septic-tank-riser-installation).
