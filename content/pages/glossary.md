## Plumbing terms

### Backflow preventer

A device that keeps contaminated water from reversing direction and entering the potable water supply.

### Cleanout

A capped access point that allows a drain or sewer line to be inspected and cleaned.

### DWV

Drain, waste, and vent piping that moves wastewater away while balancing air pressure in the drainage system.

### PRV

A pressure-reducing valve that lowers incoming water pressure to a safe operating range.

### T&P relief valve

The temperature-and-pressure safety valve installed on a tank-style water heater.

### Trenchless sewer repair

Methods that rehabilitate or replace a buried pipe with limited excavation, such as pipe lining or pipe bursting.

## Septic terms

### ATU

An aerobic treatment unit that uses oxygen and biological treatment to produce cleaner effluent than a basic septic tank.

### Distribution box

A component that divides septic effluent among multiple drain-field lines.

### Drain field

The soil absorption area where treated septic effluent is dispersed.

### Effluent filter

A removable filter at the tank outlet that reduces solids entering the drain field.

### Riser

A watertight extension that brings a septic-tank access opening closer to the ground surface.
