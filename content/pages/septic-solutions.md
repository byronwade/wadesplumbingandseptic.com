## One team for the complete septic system

Wade's works on the tank, distribution components, pumps, alarms, treatment equipment, controls, and dispersal field. That complete-system view helps separate a maintenance issue from a hydraulic, mechanical, electrical, or site failure.

## Septic services

- Inspections, assessments, and real-estate certifications
- Tank pumping coordination, filter service, and maintenance
- Camera inspection, line jetting, and diagnostics
- Tank, baffle, riser, distribution-box, and alarm work
- Drain field and leach field evaluation or repair
- System upgrades, abandonment, and replacement
- Design coordination, permitting, and compliance
- Alternative and engineered treatment systems

## Engineered systems for difficult properties

Steep slopes, limited usable area, poor soil, high groundwater, environmental setbacks, and newer treatment standards may require more than a conventional gravity system. Wade's can help move a project from site evaluation through design coordination, permitting, installation, startup, and maintenance planning.
