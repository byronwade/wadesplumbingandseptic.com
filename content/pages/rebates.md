## Lower the cost of efficient equipment

Utilities, manufacturers, air-quality districts, and local programs periodically offer rebates for qualifying high-efficiency equipment. Availability depends on the property, product model, fuel type, installation date, and program funding.

Potential programs may cover:

- High-efficiency tank and tankless water heaters
- Heat-pump water heaters
- Water-saving toilets and fixtures
- Hot-water recirculation controls
- Leak detection and automatic shutoff equipment
- Commercial efficiency upgrades

Confirm eligibility before purchasing equipment. Wade's can provide model information and installation documents, but the customer or program administrator controls approval and payment.
