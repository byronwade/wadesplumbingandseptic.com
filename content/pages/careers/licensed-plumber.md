## Job Description

Wade's Plumbing & Septic is seeking licensed plumbers to join our growing team. This position involves residential and commercial service work, installations, and repairs.

## Responsibilities

  * Diagnose and repair plumbing systems and fixtures
  * Install new plumbing systems in residential and commercial settings
  * Respond to service calls and emergency situations
  * Maintain accurate records of work performed
  * Provide estimates for repairs and installations
  * Ensure all work meets applicable codes and standards

## Requirements

  * Valid plumbing license
  * Minimum 2 years of experience in residential/commercial plumbing
  * Clean driving record
  * Strong customer service skills
  * Problem-solving abilities and attention to detail
  * Ability to work independently and as part of a team

## Benefits

  * Competitive hourly rate based on experience
  * Health, dental, and vision insurance
  * 401(k) retirement plan with company match
  * Paid vacation and holidays
  * Company vehicle and fuel card
  * Ongoing training and certification opportunities
  * Career advancement possibilities

Wade's Plumbing & Septic is an equal opportunity employer committed to creating an inclusive workplace.

Submit your resume and cover letter through our online application form or email careers@wadesplumbing.com.
