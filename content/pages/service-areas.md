## How service works in your area

1. **Confirm coverage.** Call with your city or ZIP code and we will confirm current availability.
2. **Schedule the work.** A real person listens to the issue and finds the next appropriate appointment.
3. **Get clear options.** A licensed local technician evaluates the system and provides pricing before work begins.

Regular hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Emergency calls are answered 24/7.
