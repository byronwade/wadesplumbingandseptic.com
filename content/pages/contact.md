## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get a quote?

Call or text 831.225.4344, or email the property address and a short description of the problem. Quotes are free. Many jobs need an on-site look before we can give a firm number.

### When can I schedule plumbing service?

Call 831.225.4344 Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. During office hours we triage leaks, backups, and septic alarms as the schedule allows. Outside those hours, leave a message or text with the address and what is happening.

### What should I expect on a service visit?

We confirm the address and issue when you book, diagnose on site, explain what we find in plain language, and complete agreed work with testing and cleanup. You get options, not a hard sell.

### Can I save your contact on my phone?

Yes. Under the call and email actions on the card there is an optional “Save to contacts” link. It opens a contact card with our logo, phone, email, and address.
