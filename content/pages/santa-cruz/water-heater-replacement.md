## The right water heater for the property

Wade's installs and replaces conventional tank, high-efficiency, and tankless water heaters. We review household demand, fuel, venting, gas capacity, electrical requirements, space, recirculation, drainage, and code conditions before recommending equipment.

Installation can include removal, new shutoffs and supplies, drain pan, expansion control, venting, gas work, startup, temperature adjustment, testing, and disposal as listed in the written estimate.
