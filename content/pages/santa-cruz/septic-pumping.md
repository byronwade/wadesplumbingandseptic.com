## Pumping is maintenance, not a complete diagnosis

Regular removal of accumulated solids protects the tank and drain field. The right interval depends on tank size, household use, system type, garbage-disposal use, and prior measurements.

Wade's can help locate access, evaluate lids and risers, inspect accessible baffles and filters, document visible conditions, and explain whether additional diagnostics are warranted.

Call before a backup develops if you notice odors, slow drains, an alarm, or wet soil over the system.
