Not every clog needs the same tool. Snaking (cable machines) and hydro jetting both clear drains, but they solve different problems. Choosing wrong usually means the clog comes back in weeks.

## Snaking: best for a soft blockage

A cable snake punches through or retrieves a localized clog: a wad of paper, a small root intrusion, or a soft kitchen stoppage. It is often the right first step when:

- One fixture is slow and others are fine
- The line has a cleanout and a short, simple path
- You need a fast open-up and the pipe history is known

Snaking does not wash the pipe wall clean. Grease and scale can remain, so the hole the cable made can close again.

## Hydro jetting: best for coated or rooty lines

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of the pipe. It is the better tool when:

- Kitchen lines keep clogging after recent snaking
- Camera inspection shows grease coating or heavy scale
- Roots are present but the pipe is still structurally sound
- A main line serves multiple fixtures and keeps slowing down

Jetting is not a cure for collapsed, bellied, or broken pipe. That is a repair or replacement conversation after a camera look.

## A practical sequence we use

1. Ask which fixtures are affected and how often the clog returns.
2. Open the line safely if the home needs drains working today.
3. Camera when the story smells like a recurring or structural issue.
4. Recommend jetting, targeted repair, or [trenchless replacement](/service-offerings/trenchless-sewer-line-replacement) based on evidence, not a package upsell.
