Wade's Plumbing & Septic ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit [wadesplumbingandseptic.com](https://wadesplumbingandseptic.com), request service, or contact us.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Personal Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and service address when you request services, contact us, or submit a form.
- **Service Information:** Details about your plumbing or septic system when you request an estimate or appointment.
- **Payment Information:** Payment details when you pay for services (processed by our payment processors; we do not store full card numbers on this website).
- **Usage Information:** How you interact with our website, including pages viewed and links clicked.
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, and general device information used for security and analytics.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide plumbing and septic services and customer support
- Schedule appointments and send related updates
- Respond to inquiries and estimate requests
- Process payments for completed work
- Improve our website and service experience
- Meet legal, licensing, and safety obligations

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

- Service providers who help us operate the website, process payments, or deliver communications
- Licensed subcontractors when needed to complete your job
- Authorities when required by law or to protect safety and property

## Cookies and Analytics

Our site may use cookies or similar technologies, including privacy-friendly analytics, to understand traffic and improve pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Data Retention

We keep service and contact records as long as needed for business, warranty, accounting, and legal purposes, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

## Your Choices

You may request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you, or ask us to update marketing preferences, by emailing [support@wadesinc.io](mailto:support@wadesinc.io) or calling [831.225.4344](tel:+18312254344).

## Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to homeowners and businesses, not children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
