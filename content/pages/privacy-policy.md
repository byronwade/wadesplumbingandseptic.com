Wade's Plumbing & Septic ("Wade's," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use
[wadesplumbingandseptic.com](https://wadesplumbingandseptic.com), contact us, or
request plumbing and septic service, how we use that information, and the choices
you have.

This policy applies to our website, related online forms, phone and email
inquiries, and job-related communications for properties we serve in Santa Cruz
County and selected Santa Clara County communities in California. It does not
cover third-party websites we link to.

## Information we collect

### Information you provide

When you call, email, submit a form, request a quote, schedule service, or apply
for a job, you may provide:

- Name, phone number, email address, and service address
- Details about the plumbing or septic issue, property access, and preferred
  contact times
- Photos, documents, or notes you choose to send
- Payment details when you pay for completed work (processed by our payment
  providers; we do not store full card numbers on this website)
- Application materials if you apply for employment

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, we and our service providers may collect:

- Pages viewed, referring URL, and approximate time on page
- Device and browser information, such as IP address, user agent, and screen
  size
- Cookie or similar identifiers used for essential site functions, analytics,
  and (where used) measuring unique page views on service and tip pages

### Information from service work

For jobs we perform, we may keep records needed to complete the work, follow
code and permit requirements, document warranties, and communicate about
follow-up. That can include site findings, equipment details, photos of the
work area, and invoices.

## How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Respond to inquiries and schedule service
- Diagnose issues, perform work, and provide documentation
- Process payments and send receipts or job-related updates
- Improve the website, content, and service experience
- Measure which service and education pages are useful (including unique view
  counts where enabled)
- Comply with law, licensing, insurance, and safety obligations
- Protect our customers, team, and business against fraud or abuse

We may send service-related messages (appointment confirmations, follow-ups, or
warranty notices). Marketing messages, if any, are optional and you can opt out
using the instructions in the message or by contacting us.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the site to
function, and we may use analytics tools (such as Vercel Analytics) and
first-party view tracking to understand which pages are visited. You can control
cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site
features such as remembering that you already viewed a page for unique-view
counting.

## How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with:

- Service providers that help us operate the website, email, hosting, analytics,
  payment processing, or communications (under contracts that limit their use of
  the data)
- Licensed partners or subcontractors when needed to complete a job you
  requested
- Professional advisors (for example insurance, legal, or accounting) when
  appropriate
- Government agencies when required for permits, inspections, or legal process
- Others when you direct us to, or when we believe disclosure is required to
  protect rights, safety, or property

If we are involved in a business transfer, customer information may be included
among transferred assets, subject to this policy or a successor policy with
comparable protections.

## California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California
Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to know what personal
information we collect, request deletion (subject to legal exceptions), correct
inaccurate information, and opt out of sale or sharing of personal information
for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the website to knowingly sell
or share personal information of consumers under 16.

To exercise applicable rights, email [support@wadesinc.io](mailto:support@wadesinc.io)
with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line and enough detail for us
to verify and respond. We will not discriminate against you for exercising
privacy rights.

## How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including
job records, warranty support, accounting, and legal retention requirements.
Website analytics and view aggregates may be retained in summarized form.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate
to the type of information involved. No method of transmission or storage is
completely secure. Please use care when sending sensitive details by email or
text.

## Children's privacy

Our website and services are directed to adults and property decision-makers. We
do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you
believe a child provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it
when appropriate.

## Third-party links

Our site may link to county resources, manufacturers, utilities, or other
external sites. Their privacy practices are their own. Review their policies
before providing information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at
the top will change when we do. Continued use of the website after an update
means you acknowledge the revised policy. For material changes, we may also
provide additional notice on the site.
