## Find the failure before choosing the repair

High water use, wet soil, low pressure, sewage backups, root intrusion, and recurring clogs may indicate a damaged underground line. Wade's combines symptom review, locating, pressure testing, drain equipment, and camera inspection as appropriate.

Potential solutions include targeted excavation, water-service replacement, cleanout installation, sewer repair, and trenchless replacement where the pipe and site qualify.

The process starts with phone triage, an inspection plan, and clear repair options.
