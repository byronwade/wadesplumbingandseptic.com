## Practical information to keep

This resource center collects the same core guidance used throughout our homeowner articles.

### Urgent plumbing checklist

1. Shut off water at the nearest working valve or the building main.
2. Keep people and electricity away from wet areas.
3. Photograph visible damage when it is safe.
4. Call for professional diagnosis.

### Septic warning-sign checklist

- Multiple slow or gurgling drains
- Sewage odor indoors or outdoors
- Surfacing water or unusually green soil over the field
- Pump or high-level alarm
- Backup at the lowest fixture

### Preparing for a service visit

- Clear safe access to the affected fixture or equipment.
- Secure pets and identify parking access.
- Gather model numbers, prior reports, and relevant permits.
- Write down when the symptom started and which fixtures are affected.
