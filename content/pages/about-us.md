Wade's Plumbing & Septic is a family-owned plumbing and septic company based in Ben Lomond. We serve Santa Cruz County and selected Santa Clara County communities. A short [company facts](/facts) page lists hours, license, service area, and what Wade's does not offer.

## What we do

Residential and commercial plumbing, drain and sewer work, water heaters, and septic system installation. Engineered systems are installed alongside your engineer.

## What we do not do

Wade's does not pump septic tanks. For routine pumping, hire a pumping company.

## How we work

We listen, explain what we find in plain language, and give a flat-rate price before work begins. Quotes are free. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

We hold California contractor license CSLB #1087260 (C-36 Plumbing, C-42 Sanitation System).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What work can I contact Wade's about?

Wade's Plumbing & Septic handles residential and commercial plumbing, drain cleaning, sewer line repairs, water heater work, and septic system installation. We serve homes and businesses.

### Where does Wade's provide plumbing and septic services?

Wade's serves Santa Cruz County and selected Santa Clara County communities in California. Check our service-area guide or call 831.225.4344 to ask about your property's location.

### Does Wade's install engineered septic systems?

Yes. Wade's installs engineered septic systems alongside engineers, including systems for properties where a conventional septic system will not work. The system design depends on the property's site conditions.

### Is septic tank pumping part of Wade's services?

No. Wade's Plumbing & Septic does not provide septic tank pumping. Our septic work includes installation alongside engineers. Contact a septic pumping company if you need routine pumping.

### Is there a charge for a quote?

Quotes are free. Flat-rate pricing, quoted on site before work begins. Call 831.225.4344 or use our contact page to request a quote for your project.

### When can I reach Wade's to discuss my project?

Call Wade's at 831.225.4344 Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. You can also request a free quote through our contact page.

## Commercial work

Wade's also handles commercial plumbing and septic installation for businesses in the service area. Quotes are free. Flat-rate pricing, quoted on site before work begins.

### How is pricing set for commercial work?

Flat-rate pricing, quoted on site before work begins. Cost depends on the scope of the job.

### What should I expect during a commercial service visit?

We assess the plumbing or septic issue, explain what we find, and agree on the work before we start. We aim to keep disruption to the business as low as the job allows.
