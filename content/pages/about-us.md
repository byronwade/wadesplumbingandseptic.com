## A company built to earn trust, not just win jobs

Wade's Plumbing & Septic was founded with a simple standard: do honest work, communicate clearly, and treat every property with care.

Today we serve homeowners and businesses throughout Santa Cruz County and Santa Clara County, California with practical solutions, transparent pricing, and dependable follow-through. From routine plumbing to engineered septic work, we focus on getting it done right the first time.

## Meet the people behind the standard

### Byron Wade — Founder

Byron built Wade's Plumbing & Septic around straight answers, quality workmanship, and long-term customer trust. He sets the service standards and leads company direction.

### Dave — Co-Owner

Dave co-leads operations, project planning, and field execution. He keeps crews aligned, schedules tight, and quality consistent from the first call to the final walkthrough.

## The values you can feel on every job

- **Honest recommendations:** No pressure and no upsells. You get practical options and clear pricing before work begins.
- **Licensed local team:** Licensed, insured, and code-compliant work for residential and commercial properties.
- **Fast, clear communication:** Responsive scheduling and real updates from the first call through completion.
- **Built for long-term reliability:** We design and repair systems to last, not just patch the immediate symptom.
