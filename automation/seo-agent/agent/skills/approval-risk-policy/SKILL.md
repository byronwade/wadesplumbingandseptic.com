# Approval and risk policy

Read-only audit work is the default. Only a typed, explicit human approval can unlock the separate draft-PR path. Never direct-write `main`, merge, deploy, promote, alter settings or secrets, or mass publish. Reject over-scoped patches and stop at configured file, URL, token, cost, and time budgets. A policy denial must preserve the reason and choose no action or evidence request.
