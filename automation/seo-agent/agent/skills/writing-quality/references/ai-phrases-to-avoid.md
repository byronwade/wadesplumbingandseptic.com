# AI phrases to avoid

Do not use these in drafts:

- in today's digital age
- in conclusion
- it is important to note
- game changer
- unlock the power
- delve into
- comprehensive guide to everything
- as an AI
- cutting-edge solution
- nestled in
- tapestry of
- elevate your
- seamlessly
- leverage (as a verb for ordinary use)

Prefer concrete verbs and local nouns: slope, roots, tank, drain field, permit, Santa Cruz County.
